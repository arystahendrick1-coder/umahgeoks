Our Story

GEOKS stands for Geria Olah Kreativitas Seni, a place where creativity, culture, and daily life come together. Bali is known for tourism, and Ubud is deeply rooted in art and spiritual traditions. From this spirit, Umah GEOKS was created.

This is not a hotel, but a Balinese home that welcomes guests into real daily life. Art is part of everyday routines, shaping a meaningful Balinese life experience.

Umah GEOKS is owned by I Putu Budi Santosa, a person deeply dedicated to tourism, especially in Ubud Bali. His vision is to introduce travelers to Balinese culture in an honest and meaningful way. Through this vision, he and his family continue to preserve Balinese traditions that have been passed down from their ancestors.

Around Ubud, guests can take part in purification rituals, watch traditional dance and sound performances, visit art museums, and learn various forms of Balinese art. These activities allow guests to connect with both the creative and spiritual sides of Bali.

Umah GEOKS is a place for travelers who seek authenticity, cultural immersion, and a deeper understanding of Bali through art, tradition, and daily life.

Our Offerings

AccommodationLive the Balinese Homestay Experience

Stay in a traditional Balinese homestay where guests blend naturally into Balinese family daily life. Available rooms include twin and king bed options, offering comfort while staying connected to local culture and everyday routines.

ExploreDiscover Ubud and Its Surroundings

Explore the heart of Ubud through cultural tours and iconic destinations. Guests can experience purification rituals, scenic trekking routes, art villages, and other activities that make Ubud one of Bali's most meaningful places to visit.

TransportEasy and Flexible Arrangements

Transportation can be arranged to support your journey around Ubud and beyond. From airport transfers to local trips, we help make your travel simple and comfortable.

CultureLearn and Experience Balinese Arts

Umah GEOKS is rooted in creativity and artistic expression. If you are fortunate, you may witness local art groups practicing or performing at the GEOKS amphitheater, offering a rare glimpse into living Balinese art.
